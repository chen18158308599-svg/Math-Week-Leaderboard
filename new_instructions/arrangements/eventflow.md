## Strategy and Goals:
Foot-Traffic Strategy: Use interactive mini-booths as engaging "teasers" to attract crowds, naturally guiding them into the main Exhibition which serves as the complete, in-depth showcase of the event's mathematics. 
Core Message: Showcase (Power of Math) as visually appealing and practically relevant across all university majors—not just boring paperwork. 
Reusability: Build durable, evergreen props and displays that the student council can easily reuse for future orientations and events. 
Scope Reminder: This is Math Week, not MAT Department Week. Keep the content centered on celebrating mathematics broadly rather than over-promoting our specific program.
